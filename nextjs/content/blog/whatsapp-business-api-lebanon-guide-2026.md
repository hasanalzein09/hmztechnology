## What the WhatsApp Business API Actually Is

There are three WhatsApp products, and confusing them is the #1 mistake Lebanese businesses make:

| Product | Who it's for | Automation? | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **WhatsApp (personal)** | Individuals | No | Free |
| **WhatsApp Business App** | Very small shops (1 user, 1 phone) | Basic quick replies only | Free |
| **WhatsApp Business API (Cloud API)** | Real businesses | Full automation, AI chatbots, CRM integration, multi-agent | Per-conversation fees |

The API is the only version that allows **AI chatbots, automated booking, order processing, and integration with your systems**. It's also the only version where your number can't get banned for sending automated messages at scale.

## Real 2026 Pricing for Lebanon

Meta charges per **conversation** (a 24-hour messaging window), not per message. As of 2026, Meta's conversation categories and approximate rates for Lebanon are:

| Conversation type | When it applies | Approx. cost (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| **Service / Utility** | Order updates, appointment reminders, answers to customer-initiated chats | ~$0.025–0.035 |
| **Marketing** | Promotions, offers, re-engagement campaigns | ~$0.06–0.08 |
| **Authentication** | OTP codes | ~$0.03 |

**Three things that save you money:**

1. **Customer-initiated conversations are the cheapest.** When a customer messages you first, the 24-hour "customer service window" lets you reply freely at the lowest rate — and an AI chatbot can handle the entire conversation inside that window.
2. **Free entry points.** Conversations started from a **Click-to-WhatsApp ad** or a Facebook Page CTA are free for 72 hours. For Lebanese businesses running Instagram/Facebook ads, this is the single biggest cost saver.
3. **The first 1,000 service conversations each month are free** from Meta.

On top of Meta's fees, your provider charges either a monthly platform fee (typically $30–100/month) or a small markup per conversation. ## Setup Steps for a Lebanese Business (Realistic Timeline)

1. **Day 1–2: Meta Business verification.** You need a Facebook Business Manager account and business documents (commercial registry / سجل تجاري works fine). Meta verifies Lebanese businesses routinely.
2. **Day 2–3: Number registration.** A dedicated number (a fresh SIM works — it doesn't need WhatsApp installed). The number gets registered to the API and can't be used in the normal app afterwards.
3. **Day 3–5: Display name approval.** Your business name as it appears to customers. It must match your registered business name reasonably closely.
4. **Day 5–7: AI layer deployment.** This is where the chatbot gets built: your services, prices, FAQs, booking flows — in the languages your customers actually speak.
