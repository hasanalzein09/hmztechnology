## Quick Answer

**ManyChat wins for simple marketing flows** (comment-to-DM, basic funnels, broadcasts) and costs $15–145/month. **A custom AI agent wins when conversations matter**: understanding free-text questions in any language or dialect, pulling answers from your actual business data, booking/ordering end-to-end, and handing off to humans intelligently. Many businesses start on ManyChat and migrate to custom AI once their bot becomes a sales channel rather than a gimmick — typically when message volume passes a few thousand per month or when template flows start losing customers.

## What We're Actually Comparing

This isn't "two chatbot tools." It's two different technologies:

- **ManyChat (and Wati, Respond.io, etc.):** flow builders. You design decision trees — buttons, keywords, if/else branches. The bot follows your script.
- **Custom AI agent (what HMZ Technology builds):** an LLM-based system that *understands* language, connected to your business data (services, prices, availability, orders) through tools and APIs. There is no script — there's a brain.

Both run on the official WhatsApp Business API. The difference is what happens when a customer goes off-script — which is most of the time.

## Where ManyChat Genuinely Wins

Credit where due — ManyChat is the right choice when:

1. **You need Instagram comment-to-DM automation** for growth campaigns. This is ManyChat's killer feature and it's excellent.
2. **Your flows are truly simple:** "Reply YES to get the price list" works fine as a decision tree.
3. **You have no budget for a proper build** and want something live this week.
4. **Broadcast marketing** to an opted-in list is your main use case.

If that's you, use ManyChat. Seriously.

## Where ManyChat Breaks (The Migration Triggers)

These are the exact pain points that push businesses to custom AI:

**1. The "I didn't understand that" wall.** Real customers don't click buttons — they type questions. "Do you have anything for back pain, and can I come Thursday evening?" breaks a flow bot instantly. An LLM agent parses intent, checks availability, and books the slot.

**2. Arabic and dialect support.** Flow bots don't understand language at all — they match keywords. Lebanese, Gulf, or Egyptian customers typing in dialect (or Arabizi like "bukra" or "3andkom") get nothing. For MENA businesses this alone is a deal-breaker.

**3. The maintenance spiral.** Every new service, price change, or FAQ means editing flows. At 50+ branches, the flow map becomes unmaintainable. A RAG-based AI agent updates when you update a document.

**4. No real integration depth.** Flow builders integrate via Zapier-style patches. Custom agents call your booking system, CRM, or inventory API directly — checking real availability, creating real orders.

**5. Platform pricing at scale.** Per-contact pricing punishes growth. A custom agent's running cost is mostly LLM tokens — typically far cheaper at volume, and you own the asset.

## The Hybrid Reality (What We Often Recommend)

You don't always have to choose. A common architecture we deploy:

- **ManyChat (or similar)** stays for Instagram comment automation and broadcasts
- **Custom AI agent** handles all actual conversations, bookings, and sales
- Both share the same WhatsApp number and CRM

The flow tool does marketing; the AI does business.

## How to Decide in 60 Seconds

Ask yourself one question: **"When my customer types a question my flow doesn't cover, what happens?"**

- "Nothing important — they click a button or leave" → ManyChat is fine.
- "I lose a lead, a booking, or a sale" → you need a custom AI agent.
